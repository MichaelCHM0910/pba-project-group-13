1. What Milestone 2 asks for ｜ 作業要求

English中文
A 1–2 page proposal (informal is fine) that covers:
  1. The research question in one sentence, and whether it is descriptive, predictive, or causal.
  2. The hypothesis and why it might be true (a plausible mechanism matters more than a citation).
  3. A data description: source and how it was collected, unit of observation, period, number of observations, and a variable table (name, meaning, unit).
  4. The key variables: explanatory variable / treatment and outcome, and how each is measured.
  5. What would count as evidence: which pattern supports the hypothesis and which contradicts it.
Data must be real (not synthetic Kaggle data); a Kaggle dataset is OK only if its original source is documented. Load the data in R before writing the proposal. Nothing is locked in; we can change later.		 一份 1–2 頁的提案（不用太正式），內容包括：
  1. 一句話的研究問題，並說明是描述型、預測型還是因果型。
  2. 假設，以及為什麼可能成立（合理的機制比引用文獻重要）。
  3. 資料描述：來源與蒐集方式、觀察單位、期間、樣本數，以及變數表（名稱、意義、單位）。
  4. 關鍵變數：解釋變數（或處理）與結果變數，各自怎麼衡量。
  5. 什麼算證據：什麼樣的資料型態支持假設、什麼樣的型態推翻假設。

2. Proposed question ｜ 研究問題

English中文
Which audio features of a song (danceability, energy, loudness, length, and others) predict its popularity on Spotify, after accounting for its genre and release year?

Type: predictive, with a descriptive first part. We do not claim causation: songs are not randomly assigned their features, and artist fame is not in the data.		 在控制曲風與發行年份之後，一首歌的音訊特徵（好不好跳、能量、音量、長度等）能不能預測它在 Spotify 上的熱門程度？

類型：預測型，先做一段描述型分析。我們不主張因果：歌曲的特徵不是隨機分配的，資料裡也沒有歌手知名度。

3. Hypotheses ｜ 假設與理由

English中文
H1. Danceable, loud, and shorter songs are more popular. Streaming pays per play and a play counts after 30 seconds, so shorter songs are replayed more and skipped less; danceable, loud songs fit the party, workout, and "hits" playlists that drive most listening. H1. 好跳、大聲、比較短的歌比較紅。串流以「播放次數」計酬，聽超過 30 秒就算一次播放，所以短歌會被重播更多、被跳過更少；好跳又大聲的歌適合派對、健身、熱門歌單，而這些歌單貢獻了大部分的收聽量。
H2. Instrumental songs are less popular, because vocals and lyrics are what listeners remember, sing, and share. H2. 純音樂（沒有人聲）比較不紅，因為人聲和歌詞才是聽眾記得、會跟著唱、會分享的部分。
H3. Genre and release year matter more than audio features. Spotify's score is based on recent plays, so new releases score higher, and pop has a larger audience than EDM. H3. 曲風和發行年份的影響比音訊特徵大。Spotify 的分數主要看「最近」的播放量，所以新歌分數較高；流行樂的聽眾也比電子舞曲多。

5. Evidence and first look ｜ 什麼算證據、初步結果

English中文
Supports: holding genre and year fixed, danceability and loudness have positive coefficients, duration and instrumentalness negative; and adding audio features lowers test-set prediction error (RMSE) compared with a genre + year model.
Contradicts: coefficients near zero or of the opposite sign, or no gain in test-set accuracy, meaning popularity depends on things outside the sound (artist, marketing, playlists).		 支持假設：在控制曲風和年份後，danceability、loudness 係數為正，duration、instrumentalness 係數為負；且加入音訊特徵後，測試集的預測誤差（RMSE）比只用曲風 + 年份的模型低。
推翻假設：係數接近 0 或方向相反，或加入音訊特徵後測試集準確度沒有提升，代表熱門度取決於聲音以外的因素（歌手、行銷、歌單）。
First check (already done): correlations with popularity are weak: duration −0.14, instrumentalness −0.12, energy −0.10, danceability +0.05. Mean popularity: pop 45.9, rap 41.8, Latin 41.4, rock 39.7, R&B 35.9, EDM 30.7. This hints H3 may hold, and energy goes against H1. That is fine: an honest "audio features matter little" is a valid result. 初步檢查（已做）：各特徵與熱門度的相關都很弱：長度 −0.14、純音樂程度 −0.12、能量 −0.10、好跳程度 +0.05。各曲風平均熱門度：pop 45.9、rap 41.8、Latin 41.4、rock 39.7、R&B 35.9、EDM 30.7。看起來 H3 可能成立，而能量的方向和 H1 相反。這沒關係：誠實地得出「音訊特徵影響不大」也是有效的結論。

6. Methods and limitations ｜ 方法與限制

English中文
Methods: descriptive plots by genre and year; linear regression with controls; train/test split to compare prediction models; logistic regression for "hit" (popularity ≥ 70).
Robustness: repeat without the 9% of songs with popularity 0, and within each genre.
Limitations: songs come from playlists, not a random sample; popularity is one snapshot; artist fame is missing. No personal data.		 方法：依曲風、年份做描述圖表；加控制變數的線性迴歸；切訓練集/測試集比較預測模型；用 logistic regression 預測「熱門歌」（熱門度 ≥ 70）。
穩健性：排除 9% 熱門度為 0 的歌（多是重複發行的版本）再跑一次，並分曲風各跑一次。
限制：歌曲來自歌單而非隨機抽樣；熱門度只是某一時點的快照；沒有歌手知名度。資料不含個人資訊。
